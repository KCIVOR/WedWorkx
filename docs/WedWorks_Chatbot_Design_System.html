Bold · AI chat · Mobile first

One calm, confident blue for every message, card and button in the WedWorks chatbot.

This system styles the WedWorks AI assistant used for the 10-10-10 Campaign at Kasalang Tagaytay. It is minimal and a little futuristic: a solid royal-blue header, pale blue AI replies, rounded pill controls with a soft blue shadow, and clean white cards for anything that needs a decision, such as reviewing details, paying, or checking an invitation deadline.

07. Quick replies and suggested chips

The AI can offer two kinds of shortcuts. Suggested chips are optional ideas for what to ask. Quick replies are Yes and No buttons when the AI asks a direct question.

Suggested chips
What would you like to know?
Quick replies
Can you prepare special meals, like vegetarian or halal, if couples ask?
Disabled once answered
Rules
  • Chips: 36px tall, blue text on white, pale blue border. Tapping one sends its text as your message and removes the chips.
  • Quick replies: 44px tall pills. Yes is solid blue, No is outlined. Never use red for No; it is not an error.
  • Offer at most four chips and two quick replies at once.

11. Card anatomy

When the AI needs to show structured information or ask for a decision, it sends a card instead of a plain bubble. A card is white with a thin border, a 4px tail at the top-left like an AI bubble, and an optional icon, title, text, rows and actions.

Card

Rules
  • One main action per card. Secondary actions are outlined.
  • A card that changes state (payment pending to confirmed) is replaced in place, not stacked.
  • Cards are 94% of the log width so the tail and the avatar still line up with AI bubbles.

18. Usage principles

Keep the chat feeling like one fast, friendly conversation, even when it needs a form or a payment.

Do

  • Use one blue. Let white space and the pale blue AI bubble carry the rest.
  • Put structured work (review, edit, pay, deadline) in cards, and keep conversation in bubbles.
  • Update a card in place when its state changes.
  • Keep text at 14px or larger and tap targets at 36px or larger (44px for main buttons).
  • Show the AI’s state: online, thinking (dots), writing (caret).

Avoid

  • Step counters or progress bars. This is an open AI conversation, not a fixed form.
  • Black or navy filled bubbles. Your messages are blue.
  • Red for a plain No. Red is for errors and expired states only.
  • Stacking several cards for one decision.
  • Light grey timestamps. Use the muted slate so they stay readable.

Accessibility checklist

  • Blue #2F5BEA on white is 5.5:1 and white on blue is 5.5:1, both above the 4.5:1 minimum.
  • Every icon-only button has an aria-label. Inputs have labels; errors use aria-describedby and aria-invalid.
  • Focus is always visible: a 2px blue outline, or a white one on the blue header.
  • New AI messages are announced politely; the typing state uses role="status".
  • Animations are removed when the device asks for reduced motion.
  • This system is light only. A dark theme is not included.

Implementation note: the chatbot in index.html and chatbot.html currently uses a gold palette and bubble styles of its own. To adopt this system, replace its :root variables with the tokens above, then restyle .bubble, .card-bubble, .chip, the input bar and the status pills with the wb- classes from this file. The in-chat cards map one to one: review → section 12, payment → 13, invitation and countdown → 14, payment confirmed → 15, dashboard status → 16.
