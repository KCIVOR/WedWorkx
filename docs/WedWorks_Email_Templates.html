Rules

Anatomy

  • Header: solid blue band, white W mark, “WedWorks” in DM Sans 19 bold, campaign line in pale blue.
  • Body: white card, 1px slate hairline, 20px bottom corners, 36px padding (20px on phones).
  • Top of body: one status icon or one status pill, never both colors fighting. Then a DM Sans 26 headline.
  • Details: label/value rows in a 16px rounded box, the same as the in-chat review and payment rows.
  • Action: one blue pill button per email. Optional outline button for low-stakes links.
  • Footer: why they got the email, “we only contact you by email”, address and links in slate 12px.

Email-safe rules

  • Tables and inline styles only. The small <style> block holds the phone rules (under 620px).
  • 600px max width, fluid below it. Buttons go full width on phones.
  • DM Sans and Inter load in Apple Mail and iOS. Gmail and Outlook fall back to Arial, so layouts must still work in Arial.
  • No shadows, gradients or SVG. Outlook on Windows shows square corners; that is expected.
  • Show deadlines as a fixed date and time, not a live countdown. Emails can’t update after they’re sent.
  • Light only: the color-scheme meta asks clients not to invert colors. Some Gmail apps still darken; text stays readable either way.
  • Replace the W mark with a hosted PNG logo and the example.com links with real URLs before sending.
