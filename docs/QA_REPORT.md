# WedWorks 10-10-10: End-to-end QA report (T11)

**Result: all 14 scenarios pass.** The run found **3 genuine bugs, all fixed** (listed below), and no feature was added. Everything that could not be tested here is listed plainly under "Not covered".

## How it was tested

- The app ran as it would in production: **real Apache with PHP** (XAMPP's, on its own port, using the project's real `public/.htaccess`), MySQL, `config:cache` on, multi-threaded, so simultaneous requests were genuinely simultaneous.
- **Stand-ins** were used for the two outside services that have no credentials yet: a small local server speaking HitPay's payment-request format (webhooks were signed with the salt, exactly as HitPay does), and one speaking the Anthropic Messages format. Mail went to the log mailer.
- Browser steps (registration, both chats, uploads, payment cards, My Website, polling) used the built-in browser against that stack. Everything else used real HTTP calls (curl) with real CSRF sessions and the real Hermes token.
- Deadlines were moved by editing the database timestamps (a live server cannot time-travel); every state change itself went through the app.
- Automated suite at the end: **167 tests pass** (T1 to T11).

## Bugs found and fixed (one at a time)

1. **Wrong corners on status cards and photo thumbnails.** My design tokens `r-sm` and `r-md` clash with Tailwind's own `rounded-r-sm`/`rounded-r-md` ("right corners only, 2 px and 6 px"), so the six My Website status cards had square left corners and 6 px right corners instead of 16 px all round, and the uploaded-photo thumbnails likewise (should be 10 px). Fixed by using the tokens directly (`rounded-[var(--r-md)]`, `rounded-[var(--r-sm)]`), checked at all four corners (16 px / 10 px), and a regression test fails if either class returns (proven by putting the bug back).
2. **Status cards overflowed on a 375 px phone.** The title "Website information" and the pills "Submitted · locked", "In progress (3/6)" and "Failed, retry needed" stuck out of their cards by 3 to 21 px, because the card's inner column sized to its content and the pill never wrapped. Fixed with a width-capped inner column and a pill that may wrap inside those cards (same colors, sizes and radius). Re-checked clean for all 7 states at 320, 375 and 1280 px.
3. **"Website live" email scrolled sideways on phones.** A long website address (the live email's "Your website" row) cannot break, so the email was wider than the screen. Fixed in the shared email rows (`word-break:break-word; overflow-wrap:anywhere`); verified with a 95-character address at 320, 375 and 800 px, and covered by a test.

## Findings that are not app bugs (but you should know)

- **A. Uncached `.env` under a threaded Apache.** With config caching off and XAMPP's multi-threaded Apache, one or two requests out of ~24 simultaneous ones booted with no settings (`No application encryption key`) and failed with a 500 or 419. With `config:cache`, as the deployment guide requires, 72 simultaneous sign-ups gave zero errors. It is a property of threaded PHP reading `.env` per request, not of the app. A note was added to `DEPLOYMENT.md`.
- **B. A fully-down AI provider slows each question by about 2 to 4 seconds** until the 12-call cap is reached (the connection attempt and the timeout). The chat always completes. If the AI provider has an outage, set `AI_WORDING_ENABLED=false` (or `AI_TIMEOUT=2`). A "stop trying for a while after repeated failures" circuit breaker would be a later improvement.
- **C. Limiters worked as designed during testing** (the "Pay" button limit of 20 per minute and the Hermes limit of 120 per minute both returned 429 under my own test traffic; the counters reset with `php artisan cache:clear`).
- **D. A late payment past the grace window leaves money received with no order** (scenario 6). This is the agreed behavior (no refund system): the owner finds the `HitPay payment received but not applied` log line and refunds by hand in HitPay.
- **E. Tiny cosmetic:** at 320 px (smaller than common phones) the countdown's "Seconds" label is 1 px wider than its box; it uses the design file's fixed 10 px label type, so it was left as is.

## Not covered (and why)

- **A sandbox payment against a real staging URL** (T10's acceptance): needs a public host and HitPay sandbox credentials. The signed-webhook flow was proven against the stand-in; the rehearsal steps are in `DEPLOYMENT.md` part 9. **Decision for the owner:** do this rehearsal before launch.
- **The real HitPay sandbox**: field names of the two webhook formats and the 30-minute link expiry (the app enforces 30 minutes; HitPay's own link expiry was not confirmed) should be checked with one sandbox payment.
- **The real Anthropic API**: not called (no key); request format follows the public docs.
- **A real cPanel host**, a real cron job, real SMTP delivery and spam-folder behavior: not available here.
- **Email clients**: emails were checked in a browser at phone and desktop widths, not in Gmail, Outlook or Apple Mail.
- **Outlook (Word engine)** ignores `overflow-wrap`, so a very long website address could still stretch the live email there. Realistic addresses are short.
- Performance under real load; accessibility with a screen reader; browsers other than the built-in one.

## Decisions for the owner

1. Run the staging rehearsal with HitPay sandbox credentials before going live (DEPLOYMENT.md part 9, GO_LIVE_CHECKLIST.md).
2. Keep `AI_WORDING_ENABLED=false` for launch unless you want the friendlier wording (it is optional, and Finding B explains the one risk).
3. Decide who watches the log for `HitPay payment received but not applied` after launch (refunds are manual).
