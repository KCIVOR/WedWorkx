Checked against notes/chatbot-flow-review.md, notes/hermes-journey.md and chatbot.html on 6 Nov 2026.

  1. Waitlist email does not exist. Waitlisted suppliers are told to check their email, but no waitlist email is sent. What they should be told, now that spots never reopen, is still undecided.

  4. Edit after Chatbot 2 restarts all 8 questions instead of editing one answer like Chatbot 1.
  5. No "website live" email, although My Website tells the supplier to check their email for the link.
  6. Hermes stages don't move in chatbot.html. The order stays at 1/6 until Hermes is connected.
